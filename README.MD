# **Purpose**

This document contains the following information:
* Basic Life Support (**BLS**) certification course details 
* Navigation help for the American Heart Association's (**AHA**) website, including:
    * How to select the right class
    * How to register for a class
    * Other online resources


## **Goals**

After reading, the audience will have accomplished the following: 
* How to navigate and obtain resources within AHA's website 
* An educated understanding of available BLS courses
* How to register for a certification class

## **Who is the target audience?**

This information is designed for Healthcare professionals of all backgrounds who have been required by their profession to be BLS Certified. This document also serves to benefit other members of the community who have been required by either their employers or other entities to take a BLS certification course. Many target audience members may not realize just how many certification options there are in addition to basic CPR.

 Whether the reader works in healthcare or not, this content is designated for individuals who need to register for a course and become certified. 

## **What communication problem does this aim to address?**

Individuals required to take a BLS certification course are often given little to no instruction as to which course is right for them, or even how to sign up for a course. This document provides specific guidelines to help the audience identify the course they need to take and how to register for it.

Additionally, the AHA website contains a plethora of different materials, from certification criteria to making donations to the association. Users may find the influx of content difficult to navigate, thus this document aims to offer instruction as to how to best search for needed information.

### **To be noted:**
<u> While the following information may include some clinical instructions as to perform certain life-saving measures, this does NOT replace actual clinical training, and an official certification course should be taken to fully understand this procedures.
