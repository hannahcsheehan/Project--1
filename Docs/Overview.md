# **Understanding the AHA's Website**

The [AHA Website](https://www.heart.org/en/) contains a lot of necessary information, but it can be hard for users to navigate and find the page they need. This includes searching through the multiple course options and registering for the right one.


## Which BLS Class is the right one to take?
### **How to view certification course options on the AHA Website:**
- Navigate to **Learn CPR** 
- Select **find a course** 

For Healthcare Professionals:

- Select **Healthcare professional training** and review  course options:
1. Basic Life Support (BLS)
1. Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS)
1. ACLS for Experienced Providers (ACLS EP)
1. Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS)
1. Pediatric Emergency Assessment, Recognition, and Stabilization (PEARS)
1. Stroke Recognition and Management

For non-Healthcare Professionals:

- Select **CPR, AED, or First Aid Training**
-  Select *yes* or *no* to receiving a course completion card
- If *yes*, review available course options:
1. First Aid, CPR, and AED
1. CPR and AED only
1. First Aid only
1. Pediatric First Aid, CPR, and AED
1. Bloodborne Pathogens 
- If *no*, review the available course options:
1. Adult or Child CPR (**Ages 1 year and up**)
1. Infant CPR (**Ages 0-1 year**)
1. **Both** Adult or Child CPR and Infant CPR
1. TESTING FOR EDITS
