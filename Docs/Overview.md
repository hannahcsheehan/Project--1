# **Understanding the AHA's Website**

The [AHA Website](https://www.heart.org/en/) contains a lot of necessary information, but it can be hard for users to navigate and find the page they need. This includes searching through the multiple course options and registering for the right one.


## Which BLS Class is the right one to take?
### **All Certification course options can be found on the [AHA website](https://cpr.heart.org/en/course-catalog-search):**

The AHA offers many [options for Healthcare professionals](https://cpr.heart.org/en/course-catalog-search), who may be required to obtain a particular certification:

-  Basic Life Support (BLS)
- Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS)
- ACLS for Experienced Providers (ACLS EP)
- Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS)
- Pediatric Emergency Assessment, Recognition, and Stabilization (PEARS)
- Stroke Recognition and Management

[Options for Non-Healthcare Professionals](https://cpr.heart.org/en/course-catalog-search) are also available for anyone who has been required to obtain certifications, which vary depending on the need for a course completion card:

- CPR, AED, or First Aid Training
    - First Aid, CPR, and AED
    - CPR and AED only
    - First Aid only
    - Pediatric First Aid, CPR, and AED
    - Bloodborne Pathogens 
    - Adult or Child CPR (**Ages 1 year and up**)
    - Infant CPR (**Ages 0-1 year**)
    - **Both** Adult or Child CPR and Infant CPR
